How predictions work

PredictPro's engine looks at each team's league table record — points per game, goal difference and recent form — to estimate a strength rating. The two ratings in a fixture (with a small home-advantage boost) are compared to produce win/draw/win probabilities, a likely scoreline, and Over/Under & BTTS leanings.

Confidence is simply how far apart the two teams' chances are — a bigger gap means a more one-sided prediction. Daily special picks are just today's fixtures with the highest confidence.

This is a statistical estimate based on the data you enter, not a certainty. Keep team stats up to date for better predictions. Please gamble responsibly — 18+ only where applicable.

Live data & sync

With an API-Football key added in Settings, PredictPro can pull real standings and fixtures for the leagues you track, once a day automatically (or on demand with "Sync now"). Match odds are fetched only when you open a fixture, to stay within the free plan's daily request limit.

If "Sync now" fails immediately with a network-style error (not an "invalid key" error), your browser is likely blocking the cross-origin request (CORS) rather than anything being wrong with your key. The fix is a small proxy — for example a free Cloudflare Worker that forwards your request to api-football.com and adds the right headers — sitting between the app and the API. Ask if you'd like one built for this app.
